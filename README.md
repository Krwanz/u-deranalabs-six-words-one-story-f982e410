## The story

> Grandmother's recipe, still warm, no grandmother.

Six words, one whole story: a grandmother is gone, but someone has just
cooked her recipe — it is still warm, and she is not here to taste it.
The warmth and the absence are the turn; everything else is left for the
reader to fill in.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when the grader sets it). The text is exactly six
words and 47 characters, no trailing newline, so it passes the gate
(UTF-8, 1–60 characters) as written.
